NATURAL GLOW • SELF CARE

Beauty & self-care

Simple, healthy habits that help you care for your skin, hair, and overall wellbeing.

✨

Skin basics

Cleanse gently, moisturize when needed, and use broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day.

💆

Hair care

Be gentle with brushing and heat styling, keep your scalp clean, and avoid overly tight hairstyles.

🌿

Fresh & simple

Good sleep, hydration, nutritious meals, and regular movement can support a healthy-looking glow.

🧴

Smart routine

Introduce new skincare products one at a time and patch-test products when appropriate.

🫧

Gentle care

Avoid harsh scrubs and products that irritate your skin. Comfort comes before trends.

🌙

Night reset

Create a relaxing evening routine: wash your face, moisturize if needed, and give yourself time to unwind.

Beauty advice is general information; persistent skin or hair concerns are best discussed with a qualified professional.
